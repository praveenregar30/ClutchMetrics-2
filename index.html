Questions about ClutchMetrics

ClutchMetrics is a design practicum project, so some of the answers change as the build goes on.

What does ClutchMetrics do?

It analyses basketball games and shows the results on a dashboard for coaches and players.

Which ClutchMetrics login should I use?

Coaches use the coach login to see the whole team. Players use the player login to see their own report.

Is the analysis running yet?

Not yet. The model is still being tuned, so the figures on this page are made up to show the design.

Can I sign in today?

Not yet. The two login pages are in place, and sign-in opens once the dashboards are built.
