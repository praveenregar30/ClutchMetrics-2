Questions about ClutchMetrics

ClutchMetrics is a design practicum project: a working model and website, tested on real game videos.

What does ClutchMetrics do?

It analyses basketball game videos: it tracks players by jersey number, follows the ball and finds passes, interceptions and shots, then shows them on a dashboard for coaches and a report for players.

Which ClutchMetrics login should I use?

Coaches use the coach login to see the whole team. Players use the player login to see their own report.

Is the analysis running?

Yes. Upload a game video and the model analyses its first 3 minutes in about 20 to 30 minutes. The figures on this page come from a real analysed game, Australia vs India.

Can I sign in today?

Yes. Create a coach or player account with your email. Each account sees only the games it uploaded.
